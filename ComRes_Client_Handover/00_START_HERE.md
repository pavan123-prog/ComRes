## Materials not included

This personal handover does not contain separate requester, provider, or administrator user guides; a standalone test report; presentation screenshots or a runbook; a recorded payment demonstration; or signed client-acceptance evidence. The overview and scope guide identify these gaps. Their absence is not evidence that the related testing or approval has happened.

The files linked above are the current client documents. Copies labelled as backups are kept separately for project history.
